# Market Overview

## Target Market

**Primary:** Smartphone repair shops in Malaysia (Pasar Kedai Baiki Telefon)
**Secondary:** Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines) — WhatsApp-dominant markets

### Market Size (Estimated)

| Segment | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Malaysian phone repair shops | 3,000-5,000 | Independent shops + small chains (2-10 outlets) |
| Indonesian phone repair shops | 15,000-25,000 | Massive market, WhatsApp-dominant, similar behavior |
| Southeast Asia total | 30,000-50,000 | Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam |

### Why Malaysia First

1. **WhatsApp is universal** — 97% smartphone penetration, WhatsApp is the default business communication channel. No competitor uses WhatsApp for repair shop workflows.
2. **Personal access** — Brother's 5-outlet chain in Dungun/Kerteh. Real-world testing ground, reference customer, and domain expert on call.
3. **Underserved by existing software** — RepairDesk, Orderry, etc. are US/Europe-focused. SMS/email notifications are irrelevant in Malaysia. No Malay-language support.
4. **Beachhead for SEA expansion** — Bahasa Melayu is mutually intelligible with Bahasa Indonesia (260M population). Product architecture supports multi-language from Day 1.

### Customer Profile

| Role | Pain Point | Willingness to Pay |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner (2-10 outlets)** | No visibility across outlets, manual reports, can't track technician performance | RM100-300/mo (SaaS) |
| **Shop Manager** | Time wasted on manual intake, misquotes, customer complaints | Influences owner's buying decision |
| **Front Desk Staff** | Slow intake, can't identify devices, can't answer "how much?" | End user, not the buyer |
| **Technician** | No organized queue, no diagnosis history, parts tracking is manual | End user, not the buyer |

### Competitive Position

See `competitor-analysis.md` for detailed breakdown. Key gap:

**No existing product combines: AI vision intake + voice intake + WhatsApp approval + QR receipts + mobile-first.**

All 7 major competitors (RepairDesk, Orderry, RepairShopr, RepairQ, CellSmart, Fixably, RepairX) are desktop-first, SMS/email-based, and have zero AI-powered intake.
